A note from Jessica

Becoming a mom changed how I grow a business.

Before kids, I could go to networking events, conferences, meet people for coffee, and spend hours building relationships in person.

After becoming a mom, that just wasn’t realistic anymore. I needed a way to connect with potential clients and build trust without having to be everywhere in person.

That’s how I got into webinars.

My background is in law, and over the past few years I’ve helped dozens of businesses, from law firms, accountants and financial advisors to consultants and course creators, with marketing and client acquisition. Webinars became the strategy I kept coming back to, because they turn your expertise into a real client acquisition system.

And you don’t need a huge audience, a fancy setup, or to be great on camera.

That’s exactly what I’ll be teaching in our upcoming live webinar.
