Effective date: [Month Day, Year]

This Privacy Policy explains how [Legal entity name] (“Market Movers,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses and shares information when you visit mktmovers.com and related pages (the “Site”), contact us, book a call, join our waitlist or community, use our free tools, or receive our email and text messages.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us. Name, email address, phone number, company or firm name, job title, and anything you include in forms, booking requests, waitlist sign-ups, webinar registrations or messages to us.

Information collected automatically. IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, referring pages, and similar usage data collected through cookies, pixels and analytics tools.

Information from service providers. Details from tools we use to run our business, such as our scheduling tool, our CRM and marketing automation platform, and our community platform.

2. How we use information

We use information to respond to you and schedule calls; deliver our services, webinars, free tools and community; send service, marketing and event communications you have agreed to receive; understand and improve the Site; prevent fraud and misuse; and meet legal obligations.

3. Text messages (SMS)

If you provide your mobile number and consent, we may send text messages about appointments, webinars, your inquiry, and marketing offers. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. You can also contact us at hello@mktmovers.com.

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile number or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except service providers that help us deliver messages.

4. Email

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in any email. We may still send you non-marketing messages about an active engagement, booking or account.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate (for example hosting, scheduling, CRM and marketing automation, email and SMS delivery, analytics, payments and community platforms), under obligations to protect it; when required by law or to protect rights and safety; and in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

6. Client data

When we build systems for clients, the automations, contact lists and data live in the client's own accounts. We access that data only to perform services for the client and under the client's instructions. Questions about how a client uses your data should go to that client.

7. Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to run the Site, measure traffic and improve our marketing. You can control cookies through your browser settings. [List analytics and ad pixels in use, e.g. Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, LinkedIn Insight Tag.]

8. Third-party links and tools

The Site links to third-party services, including our free tools hosted on other platforms and our community platform. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

9. Retention and security

We keep information as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, then delete or de-identify it. We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your personal information, and to opt out of certain processing. To make a request, email hello@mktmovers.com. We will verify your request and respond as required by applicable law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

The Site is intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
