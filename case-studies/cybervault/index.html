The challenge

A strong program. A marketing engine that didn’t show it.

CyberVault had almost no social presence and no consistent story to showcase student success. There was no funnel from first click to enrollment, no guide to capture emails, and no paid ads, so thousands of potential students never heard of them.

Their webinars and workshops ran without scripts or follow-up. And content had an extra constraint: the founders preferred not to appear on camera.

What we built

One enrollment engine, six parts.

01

Content without the camera

A steady stream of posts for Facebook, Instagram and other channels, including UGC-style video that keeps the brand human and relatable, without the founders on screen.

02

Sales funnels

High-converting landing pages, lead capture and multistep messaging, built to move prospects from awareness to interest, application and enrollment.

03

Paid advertising

Targeted campaigns that expanded reach, generated qualified leads and sped up enrollment opportunities.

04

Lead magnet & newsletters

A downloadable guide to attract top-of-funnel leads and build trust early, plus branded newsletters that educate, nurture and keep the community engaged.

05

Sales support

Cold calling, lead management and appointment setting, so no lead was left behind.

06

Live events

Webinar scripts, technical setup, registration workflows and follow-up sequences that raised attendance, interaction and conversion.

The results

A growth engine that’s predictable.

A consistent brand strategy and a data-driven marketing system took CyberVault from low visibility to more than 10,000 opportunities and a $1M+ sales pipeline.

Their mission, value and student success stories now come through clearly on every channel, and the system is ready to support thousands of future cybersecurity professionals.

Enrollment
