The challenge

Nobody hires an elder law attorney from an ad.

Choosing an elder law attorney is a personal decision about a parent, a spouse or your own future, and it’s often made by the whole family. People need to trust the attorney before they’ll book a call.

Solkoff Legal needed a way to earn that trust with local families at scale, and to know which dollars turned into signed clients.

What we built

One client engine, six parts.

01

Local Meta ads

Cold acquisition campaigns across South Florida for each webinar, plus a small post-webinar remarketing budget, all at about $1.88 per click.

02

Webinar funnels

Landing pages and registration flows in GoHighLevel, with thank-you page tracking that ties about 89% of registrations back to the ads. 21% of landing page visitors registered.

03

A steady webinar calendar

Seven free webinars from April to September, each with its own campaign, budget and results, so the firm always has the next one filling.

04

Follow-up tracking

Every lead is tracked from discovery call to consult to decision, and anyone with no update for 30 days is flagged for follow-up, so no one slips through.

05

Organic Facebook

41 posts, from firm news to long-term care planning tips, that reached 140,100 people and earned 175,566 media views and 135 comments.

06

A client dashboard

The firm’s own monthly dashboard: spend, registrations, attendance, discovery calls, the live pipeline and every signed client, in one place.

$30,500

Six new clients signed from April to September, with no referral or organic revenue mixed in: every one came through the webinar funnel. That’s a 4.5× return on $6,725 in ads, or about $1,120 in ad spend per signed client.
