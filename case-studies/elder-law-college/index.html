In their words

What Elder Law College says.

The challenge

High-ticket enrollment starts with one free seat.

Attorneys don’t sign up for a $5K program from an ad. They need to see Scott teach first, so every enrollment runs through a free live webinar. The college needed a steady stream of attorneys in those seats, month after month, at a cost that made sense.

And it needed to see the whole chain, from ad impression to paying student, not just clicks and likes.

What we built

One enrollment engine, six parts.

01

Meta lead-gen ads

Capture campaigns for each webinar, with a separate retargeting budget reported on its own so the cost per registrant stays honest.

02

Webinar funnels

Landing pages built to convert: 26% of landing page visitors registered. Registrations and attendance are tracked in GoHighLevel.

03

Creative that finds attorneys

Ads built on the business case for elder law, tested and ranked by sign-ups. The winner, “40 Million,” brought in 179 leads.

04

A live webinar calendar

Five webinars in one quarter (Jul 15, Jul 30, Aug 19, Sep 10, Sep 30), each with its own campaign, budget and results.

05

Organic Facebook

34 posts, reels and carousels that reached 134,072 people and earned 164,760 media views over the quarter.

06

A client dashboard

The college’s own monthly dashboard: paid funnel, cost per webinar, top creatives, organic reach and enrollment value, all in one place.

Reporting

Their own dashboard, every number in one place.

We built Elder Law College a monthly dashboard that follows each webinar from ad impression to enrollment. It shows paid performance, cost per registration for each webinar, the top creatives, organic reach and the value of every enrollment we drove.

No guessing what the ad budget did. The college sees exactly what it paid for and what it got back.
