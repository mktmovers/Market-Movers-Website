The challenge

Great attorneys. Unpredictable growth.

Content went out when someone had time. Leads arrived in bursts. The attorneys were carrying marketing, intake and follow-up on top of client work.

Cogent wanted one cohesive system: stronger visibility in their practice areas, a reliable flow of qualified leads, cleaner operations, and a team accountable for running it all, measured against revenue.

What we built

One growth system, five parts.

01

Client acquisition & paid ads

Cold email to curated lists of founders, fintechs, banks, blockchain companies and cannabis operators. Paid ads aimed at senior executives. Landing pages backed by a free guide for each practice area, so trust started before the first call.

02

Webinars

Topic strategy, registration pages, speaker prep, promotion across social, email and outreach, live hosting and follow-up sequences. Webinars became a repeatable source of new client relationships every month.

03

Content & SEO

Weekly thought leadership on LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram, weekly SEO blog posts and newsletters, plus the complete website copy and keyword structure behind every practice page.

04

Operations

CRM setup, intake workflows, a pipeline for each lead source, and email nurture flows, so every lead got a fast, consistent follow-up.

05

A fractional team

Marketing, business development, intake, billing and admin support, so the attorneys stayed on billable work while the system ran.
